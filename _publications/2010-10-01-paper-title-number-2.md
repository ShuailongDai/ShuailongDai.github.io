This report introduces a novel approach: DC Collection - DC Transmission. This method involves
collecting variable low-voltage DC electricity generated by wind or photovoltaic (PV) systems. This DC
electricity is then converted into stable medium-voltage or high-voltage DC electricity using DC/DC
converters, enabling collection and transmission without the need for a low-frequency AC link.
This report provides a comprehensive investigation into the key technologies of RE DC collection and DC
transmission systems. By examining the current state-of-the-art, challenges, advancements, and future
trends of relevant technical areas, this report aims to offer recommendations and strategies to drive
technological progress, enhance engineering practices, and stimulate industry growth. Additionally, by
analyzing case studies of demonstration systems, this report seeks to further these goals.
