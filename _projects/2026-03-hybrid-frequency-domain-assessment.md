I lead **NIA2_NESO125**, a National Energy System Operator (NESO) innovation project delivered in collaboration with Siemens. The project runs from March 2026 to March 2027.

The project evaluates and verifies a hybrid frequency-domain methodology for power-system stability analysis in networks with high penetrations of inverter-based resources (IBRs). It combines device-level impedance scans and grid-frequency sweeps, compares the results with established electromagnetic-transient (EMT) methods, and validates frequency-domain findings through time-domain simulations.

The expected outcome is a validated, scalable stability-assessment methodology supported by comparative evidence, scripts, and practical guidance. The approach aims to reduce reliance on computationally intensive EMT simulations, accelerate stability studies, improve consistency between assessments, and support earlier identification of emerging stability risks.
